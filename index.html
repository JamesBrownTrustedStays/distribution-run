TRUSTEDSTAYS
DISTRIBUTION RUN

Acquire guests with your tractor beam · Stack rate codes & accreditations · Unlock Amadeus GDS

HOW TO PLAY

You are a TrustedStays property. Your goal: acquire guests, fulfil every power-up category, unlock Amadeus GDS.

Move with arrow keys, A/D, or mouse. Tractor beam pulls guests in. Press ESC any time to return to the menu.

HOW TO SCORE POINTS

Catch guests in your tractor beam to score. The little pixel-art figures with backpacks or briefcases are guests — pull them in to add their points.

Leisure guest 1pt
Corporate guest 5pts (briefcase)
Bonus event

Sparkle bonus events drop peak-season 2x multipliers or extra guests. Rate codes (BAR/COR) boost guest values further.

WATCH FOR HAZARDS

Market factor
GDS barrier

GDS barriers (technical, legal, contract, implementation) are exactly what TrustedStays clears for suppliers — your tractor beam can't pull them in, you have to dodge them.

UNLOCK AMADEUS GDS

Fulfil EVERY criterion below to trigger the Amadeus GDS surge:

COLLECT ONE OF
  • Accreditation — collect one of: Quality in Tourism, ISAAP, or VisitBritain
  • Industry Membership — collect one of: STAA or ARL
  • Property Management System — collect one of: Hospiria or Guesty
COLLECT BOTH
  • Rate Access Codes — collect both: BAR (Best Available Rate) AND COR (Corporate Rate)
  • Policies — collect both: 24 Hour Cancellation AND 1 Night Length of Stay

"Collect ONE OF" categories drop all options together — catch any one. "Collect BOTH" categories require both items.
